Augmented Reality — The Future of Digital Interaction.

An academic exploration of augmented reality, its applications and the responsibilities that accompany immersive digital technologies.

Emerging TechnologiesResearch & AnalysisTechnology Governance

Research overview

This research explored Augmented Reality (AR) and its potential influence on how people interact with digital information and the physical environment. It considered AR's development and evolution, applications across industries, and challenges associated with adoption.

Particular attention was given to healthcare and industrial applications, including medical visualisation, manufacturing, maintenance and workplace productivity.

Key research areas

  1. Definition and development: AR concepts, development, evolution and its relationship with digital interaction.
  2. Healthcare and industrial applications: Medical visualisation, manufacturing, maintenance and workplace productivity.
  3. Digital innovation and human interaction: How AR can influence digital experiences and interaction with physical environments.
  4. Privacy and personal data: Risks associated with sensitive personal data and information collection.
  5. Biometrics and digital identity: Concerns surrounding biometric information and digital identity in augmented environments.
  6. Legal accountability and digital property rights: Responsibility, ownership and legal accountability in digital environments.
  7. Virtual harassment and digital risks: Harmful behaviour, harassment and other risks in immersive environments.
  8. Ethical technology development: Responsible innovation, ethical design and appropriate safeguards.

Findings & governance considerations

The research highlighted that augmented reality offers opportunities for innovation and improved digital interaction, but development and adoption should be supported by responsible governance and appropriate safeguards.

Legislative governance

Strengthening legal frameworks and clarifying accountability.

Industry self-regulation

Encouraging privacy-by-design, content moderation and safety standards.

User empowerment

Promoting digital literacy, transparency and user control.
